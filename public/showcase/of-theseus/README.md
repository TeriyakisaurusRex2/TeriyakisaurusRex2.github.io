Of Theseus is a top-down incremental game about special relativity. You start with a plain Newtonian ship and rebuild it, piece by piece, until you are fast enough for the strange parts of physics to show up.

## Built so far

- Newtonian flight with momentum, thrusters and a main drive you can boost
- A navigation scope that tells you when to flip and burn to stop in time
- Stations to dock at, with a ship AI that can fly the docking for you
- Missions, a research tree for ship upgrades, and a solar map you can draw routes on
- HUD bars, fuel, life support and a live g-meter

## Next

- Relativistic effects at high speed: time dilation, length contraction, Doppler shift
